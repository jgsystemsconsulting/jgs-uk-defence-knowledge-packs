# JSP 375: Management of Health and Safety in Defence

## When to use
**Prerequisites:** none. Basic workplace H&S vocabulary helps.

Use for Defence health and safety **arrangements** detail: risk assessment, workplace inspections, COSHH/DSEAR, permits, people factors, contractors, training/climate injury, and Volume 3 high-risk estate activities. For SMS Element design use `jsp-815`; for EMS use `jsp-816`.

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Handbook role
JSP 375 implements Defence H&S arrangements primarily under HSAW duties and the SofS Policy Statement, owned by Director Defence Safety. It is not the SMS (JSP 815) or EMS (JSP 816).

### Directive / Guidance
Compulsory direction versus good practice in each topic chapter.

### Risk assessment spine
Volume 1 Chapter 8 general method specialises through topic chapters and Volume 3 high-risk estate rules.

### Alternative AMC
Rare, elevated, Dir DS-approved alternative means when default policy cannot be met.

### Assurance
Application of JSP 375 is assured under JSP 815 Element 12.
