# Glossary — jsp-375

> Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Reconstructed from JSP 375 Directive V1.7 and Volume chapter themes.

- **Accountable person**: person whose terms of reference make them responsible for suitable and sufficient systems to control H&S risks in a unit/establishment context (Ch 03).
- **ALARP**: so far as is reasonably practicable — SofS Policy Statement risk objective language used in the Directive (Ch 02).
- **Alternative AMC**: alternative acceptable means of compliance approved via Director Defence Safety when default policy cannot be met (Ch 02).
- **Directive / Guidance**: compulsory direction versus good-practice guidance inside JSP 375 chapters (Ch 01).
- **4Cs**: contractor/visitor control system themes — coordinate, cooperate, communicate, control (Ch 08).
- **Permit to work**: formal authorisation system for specified high-risk tasks (Ch 06).
- **Safe system of work**: planned controls and procedures that manage assessed risk (Ch 03).
- **Safety occurrence**: incident/near-miss event subject to reporting and investigation arrangements (Ch 08).
- **Volume 3**: high-risk activities on the Defence estate layer of JSP 375 (Ch 10).
- **Workplace inspection**: planned inspection of workplace conditions and controls (Ch 04).
