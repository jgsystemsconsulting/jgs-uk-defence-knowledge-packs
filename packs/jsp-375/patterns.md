# Patterns — jsp-375

## Directive-first chapter reading
**When:** applying any JSP 375 topic chapter.
**How:** implement Directive musts first; use Guidance and annexes to achieve them; only then consider local embellishment.
**Trade-offs:** reading Guidance alone risks missing compulsory statements.

## Risk assessment then specialist chapter
**When:** a workplace activity has multiple hazard types.
**How:** run Ch 8 general assessment; pull in specialist chapters (COSHH, noise, PTW, etc.) for agent-specific controls; reopen after change/incident.
**Trade-offs:** specialist-only assessments miss interactions between hazards.

## Controlled alternative AMC
**When:** a unit cannot meet a JSP 375 policy part permanently.
**How:** exhaust controls; elevate; consult Safety Centre/SMEs; apply to Dir DS with required contents; time-limit and re-review.
**Trade-offs:** AMC ownership of residual risk stays with the applicant organisation.

## 4Cs before contractor start
**When:** visiting workers or contractors enter Defence workplaces.
**How:** communicate hazards; confirm competence; coordinate interfaces; retain control of site rules and stop-work.
**Trade-offs:** over-process small low-risk visits; under-process high-risk estate work (especially Volume 3).
