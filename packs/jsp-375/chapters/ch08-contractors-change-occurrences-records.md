## Core Idea
These chapters manage interfaces and learning systems: 4Cs control of visiting workers and contractors, safety in management of change, safety occurrence reporting and investigation, public events, animals in the workplace, and retention of records.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **4Cs system (Ch 34)**: control of contractors/visiting workers — coordinate, cooperate, communicate, control themes with hazard registers and competence expectations.
- **Management of change (Ch 35)**: safety assessment when organisations, processes, equipment, or premises change.
- **Occurrence reporting and investigation (Ch 16)**: report, record, investigate, and learn from safety occurrences.
- **Public events (Ch 37)**: H&S arrangements when Defence runs or hosts public-facing events.
- **Animals in the workplace (Ch 38)**: control animal-related workplace risks.
- **Retention of records (Ch 39)**: how long to keep H&S records, with retention table annex themes.

## Key Concepts
- **Visitor hazard communication**: contractors need site hazards and rules before work.
- **Change triggers assessment**: organisational and technical change re-open risk assessments (links Ch 8).
- **Learning loop**: investigation without corrective action tracking fails the chapter intent.
- **Records as legal memory**: retention schedules protect people and Defence evidence positions.

## Mental Models
- 4Cs fails closed when any one C is missing (especially control and communication).
- Change programmes without H&S gate reviews create latent occurrence causes.

## Key Takeaways
1. Contractors are inside JSP 375 duty, not outside it.
2. MoC is a safety process, not only project control.
3. Occurrence investigation feeds Ch 8 learn step.
4. Retention chapter governs assessment, inspection, and health records longevity.
