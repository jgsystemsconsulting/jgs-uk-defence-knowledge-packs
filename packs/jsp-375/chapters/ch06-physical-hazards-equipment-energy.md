## Core Idea
Volume 1 chapters on physical and energy hazards set Directive/Guidance arrangements for manual handling, work equipment, electricity, noise, vibration, falls and falling objects, confined spaces, pressure systems, permit-to-work, hot working, and maintenance/construction/excavation.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Manual handling (Ch 10)**: assess and control lifting/carrying/push-pull risks (including RAPP-style tools where provided).
- **Work equipment (Ch 22)**: suitable equipment, maintenance, and use controls.
- **Electrical safety (Ch 23)**: electrical work and system controls.
- **Noise (Ch 25) and vibration (Ch 26)**: exposure assessment, control, and health surveillance triggers.
- **Falls / falling objects (Ch 27)**: prevent falls from height and struck-by risks in workplace contexts.
- **Confined spaces (Ch 28)**: entry control, atmosphere, rescue planning.
- **Pressure systems (Ch 29)**: written schemes/examination themes as chapter directs.
- **Permit to work (Ch 30)**: formal control of high-risk tasks; registers of permits.
- **Hot working (Ch 31)**: ignition control for welding/cutting-type work.
- **Maintenance, construction, excavation (Ch 33)**: temporary works and dig hazards including permit-to-dig themes where used.

## Key Concepts
- **Permit as privilege**: PTW is authorization with conditions, not paperwork after the fact.
- **Exposure measurement**: noise/vibration rely on competent assessment and limits-driven controls.
- **Rescue plan before entry**: confined-space entry without rescue capability fails Directive intent.
- **Isolation and energy control**: electrical and pressure work hinge on secure isolation.

## Mental Models
- If multiple energy sources exist, permit boundaries must name them all.
- PPE is last line after elimination/engineering/admin controls in these chapters' control hierarchies.

## Key Takeaways
1. Physical-hazard chapters implement Chapter 8 method on specific agents.
2. PTW and hot-work controls gate high-risk tasks.
3. Noise/vibration link tightly to health surveillance.
4. Volume 3 adds estate high-risk rule sets on top of these baselines (ch10).
