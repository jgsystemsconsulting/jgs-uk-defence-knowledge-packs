## Core Idea
JSP 375 applies across Defence people and locations, is assured under JSP 815 Element 12, allows a controlled alternative acceptable means of compliance (AMC) route via Director Defence Safety, and points to sibling JSPs without absorbing them.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Application scope**: employed by Defence (including under 18 where applicable); working on behalf of/under supervision of Defence; all Defence activities UK or overseas; cadet/schools carve-out for own policies. [Scope]
- **ALARP objective**: SofS Policy Statement objective to minimise work-related fatalities and ill-health and reduce risks so far as is reasonably practicable. [Introduction]
- **Defence Regulators / DSA**: regulate where exemptions or unique military activity require Defence regimes aligned where practicable with statutory equivalents. [Introduction]
- **Assurance via JSP 815 Element 12**: commanders/managers/accountable persons must assure JSP 375 policy is followed, per JSP 815 Volume 2 Element 12. [Assurance]
- **Alternative AMC**: mandatory policy may be varied only through elevated, documented alternative AMC approved by Director Defence Safety after Safety Centre consultation. [Alternative AMC]
- **Coherence table**: related publications include JSP 815, JSP 816, JSP 426, JSP 376 among others named by source. [Coherence]

## Key Concepts
- **Contractors and partners**: must be competent; CO/Head of Establishment/Accountable Person ensures compliance; contract must express Defence regulator empowerment where exemptions/disapplications apply. [Introduction]
- **Employee duties**: people must be made aware of H&S duties through site procedures. [Introduction]
- **AMC application contents**: originator; reasons; exact parts and timescale (normally ≤2 years); proposed alternative mitigations; SME/Safety Centre consultation evidence. [Alternative AMC]
- **Risk ownership after AMC**: applicant owns residual risk of not following default policy once approved. [Alternative AMC]
- **Young persons carve-out**: cadet forces and Defence-run schools maintain own safety policies; may reference JSP 375 but adapt for young persons / education rules. [Scope]

## Mental Models
- Default path: comply with chapter Directive; AMC is exception management, not local preference.
- Assurance of JSP 375 is an SMS (JSP 815) duty, not a separate private regime.

## Key Takeaways
1. Scope is Defence-wide including contractors and overseas with UK-standard commitment where reasonably practicable.
2. Assure through JSP 815 Element 12.
3. Alternative AMC needs 2* elevation themes and Dir DS approval.
4. Sibling JSPs are named, not merged.
