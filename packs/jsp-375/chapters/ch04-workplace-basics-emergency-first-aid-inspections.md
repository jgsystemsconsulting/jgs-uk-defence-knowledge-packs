## Core Idea
A cluster of Volume 1 chapters sets baseline workplace H&S arrangements: emergency and disaster planning, military/civilian workplace safety, traffic management on the estate/vessels, workplace inspections, first aid, safety signs, and transfer/closure of establishments.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Emergency and disaster planning (Ch 1)**: plan for emergencies affecting Defence workplaces and activities.
- **Workplace safety baseline (Ch 2)**: military and civilian workplace safety arrangements.
- **Traffic management (Ch 3)**: pedestrians and vehicles on Defence estate/vessels.
- **Workplace inspections (Ch 4)**: planned inspection regime and MOD Form 5020-style checklist practice.
- **First aid (Ch 5)**: first-aid arrangements, notices, and related annexes/forms.
- **Safety signs (Ch 6)**: signing hazards and mandatory actions.
- **Estate transfer/closure (Ch 7)**: H&S control when establishments transfer, close, or dispose.

## Key Concepts
- **Inspection as assurance input**: workplace inspections feed local assurance and corrective action.
- **First-aid proportionality**: provision matches risk profile and population, including remote/shift contexts as chapter directs.
- **Traffic segregation themes**: separate people and vehicles where reasonably practicable.
- **Handover hazards**: closure/transfer must not orphan residual H&S liabilities or unknown hazards.

## Mental Models
- Treat inspection findings like weak signals before incidents.
- Emergency plans fail closed if never exercised or communicated.

## Key Takeaways
1. Baseline workplace chapters are compulsory arrangement building blocks.
2. Inspections and first aid are standing systems, not one-off events.
3. Estate change is an H&S project, not only property work.
4. Signs support but do not replace controls from risk assessment (ch03).
