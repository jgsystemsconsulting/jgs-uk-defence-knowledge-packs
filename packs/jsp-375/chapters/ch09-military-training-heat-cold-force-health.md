## Core Idea
Activity and climate chapters cover military training safety for land systems, heat illness prevention, cold injury prevention, and force health protection aspects of medical planning — high-consequence operationalising of risk assessment in training and deployed contexts.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Military training for land systems (Ch 40)**: H&S arrangements unique to land training systems and activities.
- **Heat illness prevention (Ch 41)**: commander's and individual's guides, WBGT/instrument themes, hydration, acclimatisation, policy compliance examples.
- **Cold injury prevention (Ch 42)**: prevention, field inspection, NFCI assessment themes, employment limitations for protected personnel.
- **Force health protection medical planning (Ch 43)**: medical planning interfaces for force health protection with supporting forms/annexes.

## Key Concepts
- **Commander and individual duties**: climate injury chapters split leader controls and personal controls.
- **Environmental measurement**: heat chapter relies on recognised thermal indices/instruments and calibration/repair themes.
- **Acclimatisation and work/rest**: pace activity to climate risk, not only to training schedule.
- **Medical planning interface**: force health protection links unit H&S to medical planning products.

## Mental Models
- Training objectives never outrank climate physiological limits in these chapters' Directive intent.
- Cold and heat are different injury mechanisms sharing the same need for early recognition and stop-work culture.

## Key Takeaways
1. Ch 41/42 are specialist risk regimes with rich annex toolkits.
2. Ch 40 binds training design to H&S arrangements.
3. Ch 43 connects medical planning and unit protections.
4. Pack summarises method; clinical protocols stay with medical authorities.
