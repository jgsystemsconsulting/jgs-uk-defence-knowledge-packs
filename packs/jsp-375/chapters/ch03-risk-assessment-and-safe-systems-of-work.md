## Core Idea
Volume 1 Chapter 8 is the core risk-assessment chapter: Directive sets compulsory risk-assessment and safe-system expectations; Guidance walks plan activities and identify hazards, evaluate risk, review, learn, and retain records, with flowchart and site checklist annexes.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Two-part chapter pattern**: Part 1 Directive / Part 2 Guidance used across JSP 375 topic chapters. [Ch 8 structure]
- **Safety risk assessment process**: structured process from planning through review and learning. [Directive + Guidance]
- **Safe systems of work**: controls and procedures that make work safe enough against assessed risk. [Ch 8 purpose]
- **Accountable person**: person whose terms of reference make them responsible for suitable and sufficient systems to control H&S risks in the unit/establishment context. [Terms]

## Key Concepts
- **Competent person**: competence aligned to recognised HSE competence ideas as updated in chapter history; assessors must be able to do the assessment tasked. [Amendment themes / terms]
- **Plan and identify hazards**: start from activities and contexts, not generic lists alone. [Guidance]
- **Evaluate risk**: estimate likelihood/severity in Defence context and decide controls. [Guidance]
- **Review**: assessments are living documents; trigger on change, time, or learning. [Guidance]
- **Learn from experience**: incidents and near misses feed assessment updates. [Guidance]
- **Retention of records**: keep risk-assessment records per Defence retention rules (see also Vol 1 retention chapter themes). [Guidance]
- **Annex tools**: flow chart (Annex A) and site risk-assessment checklist (Annex B) support consistent practice. [Annexes]

## Mental Models
- No activity plan → weak hazard ID → invalid assessment.
- Review after incidents is mandatory good practice under the learn step, not optional courtesy.

## Key Takeaways
1. Chapter 8 is the default H&S risk-assessment reference inside JSP 375.
2. Directive policy statements are compulsory; Guidance is how.
3. Annex A/B are process aids.
4. Links to occurrence reporting and retention chapters for full control loop.
