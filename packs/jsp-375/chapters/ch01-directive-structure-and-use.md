## Core Idea
JSP 375 is the Defence handbook for management of health and safety arrangements required primarily by the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974. It is a single-part publication whose volume chapters each split **Directive** (must follow) and **Guidance** (should follow).

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Directive / Guidance split**: every volume chapter states compulsory direction separately from good-practice guidance. [How to use JSP]
- **Functional ownership**: Director Defence Safety (Dir DS) is functional owner of JSP 375 under the Safety function. [Foreword]
- **SofS Policy Statement linkage**: requirement to keep people safe and protect the environment is set by the Secretary of State's Health, Safety & Environment Policy Statement. [Foreword]

## Key Concepts
- **Audience**: all Defence personnel and all who deliver Defence activities, including Military Commands, Head Office, DNO, and Enabling Organisations. [How to use]
- **Two volumes of chapters**: chapters carry policy direction and good practice for health and safety in Defence. [Structure]
- **Must vs should inside chapters**: Directive = must; Guidance = should. [How to use]
- **Not a substitute for SMS policy**: JSP 815 sets the Defence Safety Management System; JSP 375 supplies arrangements detail.

## Mental Models
- Open the matching topic chapter; read Directive first, then Guidance.
- If the question is SMS Element design, prefer JSP 815; if it is workplace H&S arrangement detail, stay in JSP 375.

## Key Takeaways
1. JSP 375 = H&S arrangements handbook under HSAW-driven Defence policy.
2. Dir DS owns the JSP.
3. Chapter structure is Directive + Guidance.
4. Related SMS/EMS JSPs are separate publications.
