## Core Idea
Volume 3 provides deeper arrangements for high-risk activities on the Defence estate: common requirements plus topic chapters on electricity, mechanical systems, petroleum installations, confined spaces, working at height, and gas — each with associated safety rule-book practice for persons in charge.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Volume 3 common requirements (Ch 2)**: shared rules that underpin topic chapters.
- **Topic high-risk chapters**: electricity; mechanical systems; petroleum installations; confined spaces; working at height; gas — each pairing policy chapter and person-in-charge safety rule book themes.
- **Person in charge / safety rule books**: authorised people control work under documented rule-book disciplines.

## Key Concepts
- **Estate high-risk vs Vol 1 baseline**: Volume 1 gives general arrangements; Volume 3 tightens controls for named high-risk estate systems.
- **Authorisation to control work**: only competent persons in charge operate under rule books.
- **System-specific isolation and permits**: each energy/system type has tailored isolation and documentation expectations.
- **Revised November 2020 baseline**: current public attachment set for several Vol 3 chapters carries that revision stamp; always prefer latest GOV.UK file when applying operationally.

## Mental Models
- Do not use Vol 1 confined-space chapter alone when Vol 3 estate confined-space rules apply to the system class.
- Rule books without common-requirements context miss shared controls.

## Key Takeaways
1. Volume 3 is the high-risk estate layer of JSP 375.
2. Common requirements apply across topic chapters.
3. Safety rule books operationalise person-in-charge control.
4. This pack maps structure and duties; it is not a substitute for the full rule-book text on the job.
