## Core Idea
These chapters control chemical and biological workplace agents: dangerous substances/explosive atmospheres (DSEAR), COSHH-style hazardous substances management including assessor competence themes, legionella control in water systems, and asbestos management.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **DSEAR arrangements (Ch 9)**: identify dangerous substances/explosive atmospheres and control ignition and release risks.
- **Hazardous substances / COSHH (Ch 11)**: assess substances, select controls, maintain registers and monitoring as required; COSHH assessor competence requirements appear in annex themes.
- **Legionella (Ch 32)**: manage legionella risk in Defence water systems.
- **Asbestos (Ch 36)**: identify, manage, and control asbestos-containing materials and work.

## Key Concepts
- **Assessment before work**: no substance work without suitable assessment and controls.
- **Competence to assess**: COSHH assessor competence is specified theme, not informal nomination.
- **Registers and monitoring**: master registers and exposure monitoring records support health surveillance links (see people-factors chapter).
- **Fabric and water systems**: asbestos and legionella are estate-condition risks as much as task risks.

## Mental Models
- Substance risk is lifecycle: store, use, spill, dispose, maintain.
- DSEAR is atmosphere and ignition control, not only COSHH labelling.

## Key Takeaways
1. Ch 11 is the general hazardous-substance spine; Ch 9 specialises explosive atmospheres.
2. Legionella and asbestos need specialist estate controls.
3. Forms (for example MOD Form 5011 family) support assessment recording; pack describes role, not form field dumps.
4. Health surveillance interfaces with occupational health chapter themes.
