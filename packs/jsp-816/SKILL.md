# JSP 816: Defence Environmental Management System (EMS) Framework

## When to use
**Prerequisites:** none. Basic EMS vocabulary (aspect, impact, assurance) helps; glossary defines Defence-specific terms.

Use for questions about how UK Ministry of Defence structures environmental protection management systems: Framework authority, 12 Elements and Expectations, assurance stages, self-assessment toolkit role, and how EMS relates to safety SMS without replacing it.

## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### Defence EMS in one view
JSP 816 sets the Defence Environmental Management System Framework under the SofS HS&EP Policy Statement. Each Defence organisation builds its own EMS against 12 Elements. Dir CCE owns the Framework line. Aligned with ISO 14001:2015 but tailored for Defence; pack never reproduces ISO text.

### PDCA and hierarchy
Plan-Do-Check-Act organises the Framework. Elements hold Expectations; Expectations hold Performance Statements across assurance maturity.

### 12 Elements (titles)
E1 Leadership, Governance and Culture; E2 Organisation and Dependencies; E3 Legislation, Policy, Regulations and Guidance; E4 Aspects, risk and impact, mitigation and opportunities; E5 Supervision, Contracting and Control of Activities; E6 Personnel Competence, Resources and Training; E7 Equipment Design, Manufacture and Maintenance; E8 Infrastructure Design, Build and Maintenance; E9 Performance, Management Information and Reporting; E10 Incident Management and Continual Improvement; E11 Communications and Stakeholder Engagement; E12 Assurance (Annex A toolkit).

### Must / should
Must = compulsory; should = best practice.

### Assurance stages
Sequential and cumulative maturity language from weak/limited through moderate to substantial.
