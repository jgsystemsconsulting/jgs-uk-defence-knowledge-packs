# Glossary — jsp-816

> Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Reconstructed definitions from JSP 816 V1.2 Dec 24 themes; full master glossaries remain in source.

- **Assurance stages**: sequential EMS maturity levels (from weak/no assurance through limited and moderate to substantial); later stages build on earlier ones (Ch 02, Ch 10).
- **Best Practicable Environmental Option (BPEO)**: mitigation choice among practicable environmental options under Element 5 (Ch 06).
- **Defence EMS Framework**: JSP 816 overarching environmental management system policy for Defence (Ch 01-02).
- **Defence Organisation**: Military Commands, TLBs, DNO, and Enabling Organisations collectively (Ch 02).
- **Environmental aspect**: element of activities, products, or services that interacts with the environment (Ch 05).
- **Environmental case**: structured environmental argument/evidence across acquisition lifecycle where required (Ch 05).
- **Expectation**: numbered activity expected inside an Element's arrangements (Ch 02).
- **Must**: compulsory requirement in JSP 816 (Ch 02).
- **Organisational EMS**: Defence organisation's own environmental management system aligned to the Framework (Ch 01-03).
- **Performance Statement**: articulation of how Expectation compliance and improvement are demonstrated across assurance maturity (Ch 02).
- **Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA)**: four-stage management system cycle used by the Defence EMS Framework (Ch 02).
- **Should**: non-compulsory best practice in JSP 816 (Ch 02).
- **Three Lines of Defence (1LOD/2LOD/3LOD)**: assurance model used in Element 12 (Ch 10).
