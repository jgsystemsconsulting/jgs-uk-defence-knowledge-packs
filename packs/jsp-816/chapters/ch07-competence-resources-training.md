## Core Idea
Element 6 ensures the EMS is staffed and skilled: resources, clear responsibilities and delegation, people development, training programmes, and competency assessment.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Element 6 Personnel Competence, Resources and Training**: Expectations E6.1-E6.5.

## Key Concepts
- **Resources (E6.1)**: sufficient people and means to run EMS duties (links to E2.3).
- **Responsibilities, accountabilities, and delegation (E6.2)**: people understand EMS roles and delegated limits.
- **People development (E6.3)**: development paths exist for environmental competence.
- **Training programmes (E6.4)**: training is planned, delivered, and relevant to role risk.
- **Competency assessment (E6.5)**: competence is assessed, not assumed from attendance alone.

## Mental Models
- Training without assessment is incomplete under E6.5.
- Resource cuts show first as E6.1 failure, then as E5 control failures.

## Key Takeaways
1. Competence is assessed capability, not a course certificate alone.
2. EMS roles need named people and time.
3. Delegation competence is shared with Element 5.
4. Development and training are separate Expectations.
