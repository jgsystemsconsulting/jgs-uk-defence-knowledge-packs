## Core Idea
Element 3 requires Defence organisations to know applicable environmental compliance obligations, turn them into local policy and guidance, communicate requirements, keep policies under review, and control exemptions, disapplications, and derogations.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Element 3 Legislation, Policy, Regulations and Guidance**: Expectations E3.1-E3.6 (compliance obligations; compliance policy; local policy and guidance; communicating requirements; review of policies; exemptions/disapplications/derogations).

## Key Concepts
- **Compliance obligations (E3.1)**: identify and maintain the set of legal and Defence regulatory environmental obligations that apply.
- **Compliance policy (E3.2)**: organisational policy states how compliance is achieved.
- **Local policy and guidance (E3.3)**: local documents implement Framework and higher policy in context.
- **Communicating requirements (E3.4)**: people who must comply can access the requirements that apply to them.
- **Review (E3.5)**: policies and guidance are reviewed so they stay current.
- **Exemptions, disapplications, derogations (E3.6)**: where statute or policy is disapplied or exempted, controls and records follow the authorised route; this pack does not replace legal advice.

## Mental Models
- Build the obligations register before writing local procedures.
- Treat exemption routes as exceptional and time-bounded, with residual risk controls.

## Key Takeaways
1. E3 is the compliance spine of the EMS.
2. Communication of requirements is an Expectation, not a side task.
3. Exemption-like routes need explicit control under E3.6.
4. Related detailed EP topic policy may sit in other JSPs (for example JSP 418); this pack stays on JSP 816.
