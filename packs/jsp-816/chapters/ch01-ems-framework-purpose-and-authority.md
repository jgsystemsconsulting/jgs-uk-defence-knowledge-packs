## Core Idea
JSP 816 is the overarching authority for environmental management across Defence. It sets goals and guidance so each Defence organisation can build an Environmental Management System (EMS) tailored to its business, under the Secretary of State's Health, Safety and Environmental Protection Policy Statement.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Defence EMS Framework**: overarching policy for environmental protection management systems across Defence organisations. [Preface Purpose 1-2]
- **SofS Policy Statement authority**: the Framework takes authority from the Secretary of State's Policy Statement on Health, Safety and Environmental Protection in Defence. [Authority]
- **12 Elements with must/should**: direction that must be followed and guidance/good practice that should be followed sit in the corresponding 12 Elements. [Purpose 3]

## Key Concepts
- **Organisational EMS**: each Defence organisation develops and implements its own environmental protection management system tailored to its business, relating back to the Framework. [Purpose 1-2]
- **Jurisdiction**: references to legislation generally mean UK law; overseas activity still considers latest Defence policy, UK standards guidance, and host-nation environmental expectations where not aligned. [Jurisdiction]
- **Disclaimer**: nothing in the Framework removes the duty to comply with applicable statute, the SofS Policy Statement, other JSPs, or Defence Regulations. [Disclaimer]
- **Document owner**: Director Climate Change and Environment (CCE); annual review cadence. [Further advice]

## Mental Models
- Use JSP 816 when the question is environmental protection management system design or Element expectations, not occupational safety arrangements (those live in JSP 815 / JSP 375).
- Treat Framework text as goals and structure; implement detail lives in the 12 Element chapters and related EP policy such as JSP 418.

## Key Takeaways
1. JSP 816 is the Defence EMS Framework, not a single-site procedure manual.
2. Authority flows from the SofS HS&EP Policy Statement.
3. Every Defence organisation is expected to run its own EMS against the Framework.
4. "Must" is compulsory; "should" is best practice (defined in Framework terms).
5. Compliance with other law and JSPs is not waived by reading this pack.
