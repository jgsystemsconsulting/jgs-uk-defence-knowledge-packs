## Core Idea
Element 1 sets tone from the top, accountabilities, leadership visibility, strategic environmental objectives, and culture. Element 2 requires an organisational EMS with clear roles, resources, information sharing, consultation, change control, and interface management.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Element 1 Leadership, Governance and Culture**: Expectations E1.1-E1.6 (tone at the top; continual improvement; accountabilities; leadership visibility; strategic objectives; culture and behaviour).
- **Element 2 Organisation and Dependencies**: Expectations E2.1-E2.8 (EMS in place; roles; resources; information sharing; environmental management standards; external consultation; structural change; dependencies and interfaces).

## Key Concepts
- **Tone at the top (E1.1)**: senior leaders set and communicate environmental policy intent.
- **Continual improvement (E1.2)**: leadership drives improvement cycles, not one-off compliance.
- **Accountabilities (E1.3 / E2.2)**: responsibilities and accountabilities are defined and known.
- **Leadership visibility (E1.4)**: leaders are seen engaging on environmental performance.
- **Strategic objectives (E1.5)**: environmental objectives align with Defence and organisational strategy.
- **Culture and behaviour (E1.6)**: expected behaviours support the EMS.
- **Organisational EMS (E2.1)**: documented EMS arrangements exist and operate.
- **Resources (E2.3)**: people, time, and means are allocated to EMS duties.
- **Dependencies and interfaces (E2.8)**: external and internal interfaces that affect environmental outcomes are identified and managed.
- **Change to structure (E2.7)**: organisational change is assessed for EMS impact.

## Mental Models
- If leadership artefacts exist but interfaces are unmanaged, fix Element 2 before claiming Element 1 maturity.
- Resource shortfalls show up as E2.3 and later as competence gaps in Element 6.

## Key Takeaways
1. E1 is about leadership system behaviour, not a poster policy alone.
2. E2 makes the EMS a managed organisational system with interfaces.
3. Consultation and information sharing are explicit Expectations, not optional extras.
4. Structural change without EMS impact assessment fails E2.7.
