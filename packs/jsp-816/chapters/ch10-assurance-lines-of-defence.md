## Core Idea
Element 12 requires risk-based assurance of the EMS through three lines of defence, external assurance where applicable, audit process discipline, annual self-assessment, leadership review, and corrective action.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Element 12 Assurance**: Expectations E12.1-E12.5 (1LOD risk-based mechanisms; enable 2LOD/3LOD; annual self-assessment; leadership review of EMS; corrective action).
- **Three Lines of Defence model**: first line delivers and self-checks; second line function assurance; third line independent audit. [Element 12]
- **Risk-based assurance**: assurance effort follows environmental risk, not uniform sampling alone. [General assurance process]

## Key Concepts
- **1LOD mechanisms (E12.1)**: Defence organisation runs regular risk-based first-line assurance.
- **Enable 2LOD and 3LOD (E12.2)**: second- and third-line assurance can operate (access, scope, independence as required).
- **Annual self-assessment (E12.3)**: yearly structured self-assessment against the Framework/Elements.
- **Leadership review (E12.4)**: leaders review EMS performance and assurance outcomes.
- **Corrective action (E12.5)**: findings drive correction and improvement, not filing only.
- **External assurance**: outside parties may assure where required; total assurance considers internal and external views. [Element 12 themes]
- **Audit process**: planned, conducted, reported, and followed up. [Audit process]

## Mental Models
- Self-assessment without corrective action fails E12.5.
- Substantial assurance language in the Framework is cumulative with earlier stages (see ch02).

## Key Takeaways
1. Assurance is a system (1/2/3LOD), not a single annual audit.
2. Annual self-assessment is mandatory theme under E12.3.
3. Leadership review binds assurance to governance (links E1).
4. Toolkit detail for self-assessment sits in Annex A (ch11).
