## Core Idea
Annex A provides the Environmental Protection Self-Assessment Toolkit used to structure annual (and risk-based) self-assessment against JSP 816 Element expectations, producing evidence and scores that feed Element 12 assurance and leadership review.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Environmental Protection Self-Assessment Toolkit (Annex A)**: spreadsheet toolkit issued with Element 12 for organisational self-assessment against EMS Expectations.
- **Link to E12.3**: annual self-assessment Expectation is the policy hook the toolkit implements.

## Key Concepts
- **Toolkit role**: operationalises self-assessment; it does not replace Element narrative or legal duties.
- **Evidence and scoring**: organisations record evidence against expectations and derive assurance-oriented results for leadership and 2LOD/3LOD use.
- **Formats**: published as ODS and XLSX attachments beside Element 12 on the JSP 816 publication set.
- **Not a parallel standard**: toolkit follows JSP 816 Elements/Expectations; it is not ISO 14001 audit checklist text.

## Mental Models
- Complete toolkit rows with real evidence references; empty green scores fail honest E12.3.
- Feed toolkit outputs into E12.4 leadership review and E12.5 corrective action.

## Key Takeaways
1. Annex A is the self-assessment workbench for Element 12.
2. Use current published toolkit files from the JSP 816 attachment set.
3. Toolkit output is input to assurance, not the end of assurance.
4. This pack describes method; it does not embed spreadsheet cell values or macros.
