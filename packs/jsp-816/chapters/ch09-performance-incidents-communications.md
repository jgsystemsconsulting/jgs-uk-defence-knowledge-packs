## Core Idea
Elements 9-11 close the Check/Act and engagement loops: monitor and review environmental performance and management information; report, record, investigate, and learn from incidents with emergency/continuity plans; identify and engage stakeholders, keep information accessible, and enable feedback and anonymous concern raising.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Element 9 Performance, Management Information and Reporting**: Expectations E9.1-E9.5 (monitor; review; MI review; leadership decisions; document storage and disposal).
- **Element 10 Incident Management and Continual Improvement**: Expectations E10.1-E10.5 (reporting; recording; investigation; actions and learning; emergency and business continuity plans tested).
- **Element 11 Communications and Stakeholder Engagement**: Expectations E11.1-E11.5 (identify; engage; collaborate; access information; feedback and anonymous concerns).

## Key Concepts
- **Performance monitoring and review (E9.1-E9.2)**: measure what matters for environmental outcomes and review it.
- **Management information to leadership (E9.3-E9.4)**: leaders decide using environmental MI.
- **Document storage and disposal (E9.5)**: EMS records controlled through life.
- **Open reporting culture (E10.1)**: incidents and near misses can be raised.
- **Investigation and learning (E10.3-E10.4)**: find causes, implement actions, feed improvement.
- **Emergency and continuity plans tested (E10.5)**: plans exist and are exercised.
- **Stakeholder system (E11.1-E11.3)**: know who matters, engage, collaborate.
- **Access and speak-up (E11.4-E11.5)**: people can get environmental information and raise concerns, including anonymously.

## Mental Models
- If MI never changes leadership decisions, E9.4 is failing even if dashboards exist.
- Incident learning without action tracking is incomplete E10.4.

## Key Takeaways
1. Performance, incidents, and communications are three linked control loops.
2. Testing emergency plans is an Expectation, not a paper exercise once.
3. Anonymous concern routes are required under E11.5 themes.
4. Record control (E9.5) supports assurance evidence.
