## Core Idea
Elements 7 and 8 apply EMS thinking across equipment and infrastructure lifecycles: identify risks and impacts, mitigate, comply with statute and Defence regulation, maintain and operate, control physical change, manage supply-chain dependencies, learn lessons, and (for equipment) manage systems integration risk.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Element 7 Equipment Design, Manufacture and Maintenance**: Expectations E7.1-E7.8.
- **Element 8 Infrastructure Design, Build and Maintenance**: Expectations E8.1-E8.7 (infrastructure counterpart without a separate E8 integration twin of E7.8).

## Key Concepts
- **Lifecycle risks and impacts (E7.1 / E8.1)**: environmental issues from concept through disposal/demolition.
- **Mitigation (E7.2 / E8.2)**: controls proportionate to lifecycle risk.
- **Compliance (E7.3 / E8.3)**: statute and Defence regulation for equipment/infrastructure.
- **Maintenance and operation (E7.4 / E8.4)**: in-service controls stay effective.
- **Physical changes (E7.5 / E8.5)**: modifications re-enter risk assessment.
- **Supply chain (E7.6 / E8.6)**: supplier and contractor environmental dependencies managed.
- **Lessons learned (E7.7 / E8.7)**: feedback into design and maintenance.
- **Equipment/systems integration risk (E7.8)**: integration introduces combined environmental risk to manage.

## Mental Models
- Treat infrastructure as a full lifecycle EMS object, not only a construction project.
- Supply-chain environmental risk is in-Element, not only procurement hygiene.

## Key Takeaways
1. E7 and E8 mirror each other with equipment-specific integration emphasis on E7.8.
2. Change and supply chain are first-class Expectations.
3. Lessons learned close the lifecycle loop.
4. Detailed topic rules may sit in other EP publications; this pack stays on JSP 816 Element intent.
