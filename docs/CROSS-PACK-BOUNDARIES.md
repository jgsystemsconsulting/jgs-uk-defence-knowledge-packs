# Cross-pack boundaries

UK defence publication one content owner, repo, once publication vetted and packaged. SE and nuclear do not carry publication content pack. Topic-name overlap allowed. Source-document overlap not. sibling may name canonical slug and repo. sibling must not install same-slug content copy.

| Topic | uk-defence owns | SE may keep | Nuclear may keep | Must not |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Safety cases | JSP 815 Element 4; JSP 376 once moved | Safety-case method sources other JSPs | NRC practice already in nuclear repo | A second content pack JSP 815 or JSP 376 |
| Assurance | JSP 815 Element 12, Annex H, and Annex G if folded in | Assurance method other sources | No JSP text | A second content pack JSP 815 |
| Risk | JSP 815 Element 4 defence risk vocabulary; JSP 376 once moved | Risk method other sources | No JSP text | A second content pack those JSPs |
| HFI | JSP 912 once moved | HFI sources are not JSP 912 | No JSP 912 text | A content copy of JSP 912 |
| AI safety | JSP 936 once moved | AI or systems-engineering sources are not JSP 936 | No JSP 936 text | A content copy JSP 936 |
| Software assurance | A UK defence source only step 1. DEF-STAN 00-051 candidate and title unconfirmed | SE signposts excluded avionics standards; no DEF-STAN text | No DEF-STAN text | DEF-STAN chapters before recorded cover licence |

ISO 45001 text excluded in every sector and signposted only here (`iso-45001-signpost`). JSP 814 and DEF-STAN 00-970 are not given pack in pass. nuclear repo was not edited because 2026-11-22 fleet survey found no UK defence content there.
